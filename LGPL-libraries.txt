LGPL libraries used by the G'MIC OpenFX plug-in
================================================

The plug-in downloads some filter data with libcurl, which uses these libraries, under the GNU
Lesser General Public License (LGPL), as separate shared libraries:

- libidn2 (LGPL v3 or later, or GPL v2 or later)
- libunistring (LGPL v3 or later, or GPL v2 or later)
- libintl, from GNU gettext (LGPL v2.1 or later)
- libiconv (LGPL v2.1 or later)

They are dynamically linked (never statically), so they can be replaced by other builds of the
same libraries, with the same ABI (major version):

- macOS: they are in GMIC.ofx.bundle/Contents/Libraries. Replace the .dylib file, then sign the
  bundle again (ad hoc), as macOS requires on arm64:
      codesign -s - --force --deep GMIC.ofx.bundle
- Windows: they are in GMIC.ofx.bundle/Contents/Win64. Replacing the .dll file is enough.
- Linux: they are not bundled; the plug-in uses the system's libraries.

The sources of these libraries are available from https://www.gnu.org/software/libidn/,
https://www.gnu.org/software/libunistring/, https://www.gnu.org/software/gettext/ and
https://www.gnu.org/software/libiconv/.
